Rumble Road – Privacy Policy

App: Rumble Road (Android, package com.rd.rumbleroad) · Effective date: 12 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Rumble Road game collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. Rumble Road is a single-player motorbike racing game. It has no user accounts, no sign-in and no in-app purchases.

1. Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress only on your own device: in-game cash, level, bikes and weapons you own, your chosen rider name and gender, your number plate text and your best lap times. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its app data deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows a banner advertisement during races, provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID);
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • ad interactions (views and taps) and diagnostic data.

This information is collected and used by Google under its own policies, including for personalised advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device). Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), you will be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.

3. Permissions

  • Internet and network state – to load advertisements.
  • Advertising ID – used by Google AdMob as described above.
  • Wake lock / foreground service – used by the game engine and ads SDK to keep the screen and ads working while you play.

The game does not access your contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone or precise location.

4. Information we do not collect

We, the developer, do not collect, store or sell any personal information. We do not run our own servers or analytics for this game.

5. Children

Rumble Road is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Data security and retention

Game progress stays on your device until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Data collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's policies.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
